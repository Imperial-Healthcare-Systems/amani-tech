## 1. Brand Messaging

### Positioning

Amani Tech is a staffing and recruitment partner that connects skilled professionals with employers across IT and non-IT roles. We combine a modern job platform with human recruiters who actually read every application.

### Brand promise

**Connecting people with opportunity.**

### One-line description

Amani Tech helps job seekers find genuine opportunities and helps employers hire the right people — across IT and non-IT roles, on contract or permanent terms.

### Tone of voice

- **Confident, not boastful.** State what we do; do not inflate.
- **Human.** Write like a recruiter talking to a candidate, not a brochure.
- **Clear.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No jargon.
- **Respectful of time.** Every section earns its place by answering a question.

Avoid: "revolutionizing", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "world-class", "AI-powered" (unless the feature is real), "dream job".

### The five questions every section must answer

| Question | Audience |
|---|---|
| Can Amani Tech help me find a job? | Candidate |
| Can Amani Tech help me find qualified staff? | Employer |
| Why should I trust Amani Tech? | Trust |
| Why Amani Tech and not someone else? | Differentiation |
| What should I do next? | Conversion |
